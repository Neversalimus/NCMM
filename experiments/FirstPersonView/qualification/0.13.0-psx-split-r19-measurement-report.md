# First Person View 0.13: matched native measurements

This report records controlled disposable native SDL fixtures. It is not a manual gameplay session or qualification of the final rebuilt installer.

Four variants use the same game data, 107-file UDP asset set, quality, seed and primary frame timers. Candidate runs additionally collect CPU/readback/staging diagnostics; their overhead remains in the recorded timings and is not subtracted.

| D | Original native draw; four-stream hashing, retained staging, exact cell reuse and separately submitted required frame copies | PSX 0.13 |

Rejected for promotion: correct queued-data handling and shorter command-record time did not establish stable complete-frame improvement. In the same-module old/split/old controls, the city wait shifted into Present. In this full comparison, forest warm median is 15.621 ms with PSX versus 7.663 ms with old arms on the same new Host; multi-level warm is 14.640 versus 10.557 ms. At viewport 1768x1084, none of the ten PSX scene first-frame medians is below 15 ms; 231 of 640 individual first frames are at most 15 ms. A/B are retained earlier controls, and differences between separate native runs do not isolate GPU execution or prove a unique root cause. Both new 80-record runs and all unfavorable samples are preserved. No static arm geometry retransmission or quality reduction was introduced. The prior source3e9 remains the main PR candidate, with its separate ordinary-rotation performance limitation still open.

Each variant contains all 80 scene/viewport/renderer combinations: 10 scenes, four viewports and CPU/GPU; 64 native steps and 96 cold plus 96 warm turns per record. The first-render timer includes native tile preparation, world extraction, rendering and SDL presentation. Move-to-ready also includes the preceding native move and native cache refresh.

The 15ms objective must be assessed per scene; this report does not turn a successful evidence check into a universal latency claim. A 64-step p99 is a coarse tail estimate. Initial record rendering does not imply a cold process/device cache. Phase arrays have exclusive outer scopes and nested diagnostics; their medians must not be added. CPU timers around GPU work measure preparation/command recording, not GPU execution. Confirmed punches precede the move-ready timer; native combat action timing is covered separately by the gameplay audit. Both binaries and all payload/weather/UDP identities were verified before comparison. Final installer hashes may differ after a rebuild of identical runtime source.
